Inkreel privacy policy

Inkreel is a personal tool that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Inkreel accesses

Inkreel uses YouTube API Services. When the owner signs in with Google, Inkreel is given permission to upload videos to that YouTube channel, to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist and visibility, and to read basic channel information and the status of uploaded videos.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored in a private file on the owner's computer and are used only to make those requests to YouTube. Inkreel has no server of its own: no Google account data, channel data or video data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only recipient of the data is YouTube, when a video is uploaded at the owner's request.

What is not collected

Inkreel collects no analytics, shows no advertising, and does not sell, share or transfer any data.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by disconnecting the channel inside Inkreel, which deletes the stored tokens. Deleting Inkreel's data folder removes everything it has stored.

Terms that also apply

By using the upload feature, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
