Inkreel

Inkreel is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It writes and produces narrated, illustrated story videos and can upload the finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It is not a public service: it has no accounts, no sign-up, and nothing to download here. The only person who signs in with Google is the owner of the channel, in order to let the tool upload their own videos.
